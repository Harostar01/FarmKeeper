Backup & Restore

Move your FarmKeeper records between devices or website versions.

How it works
  1. On your old FarmKeeper site, choose Export Backup.
  2. On this new site, choose Import Backup and select that backup file.
  3. Your farm records will be restored on this site.

⚠️ Importing a backup replaces the records currently stored on this site. Export this site's data first if you want to keep it.
